Milktoast

Mild by design. Milktoast rewraps Matroska files so QuickTime Player can play them — no re-encoding, no new player to learn.

Download for macOS Requires macOS 14.0 or later • free and open source

An .mkv, handed to macOS

🍞

Ready in Seconds

A 2.3 GB, hour-long episode is ready in under two seconds. The streams are copied, not re-encoded — it runs at the speed of your disk.

📺

QuickTime Does the Playing

AirPlay, Picture in Picture, media keys, trimming, and the track menus are all the native ones you already know. Milktoast gets out of the way.

🎚️

Only Converts What It Must

HEVC is re-tagged so it stops playing black. DTS and TrueHD keep their surround channels. FLAC stays lossless as ALAC. Everything else is copied untouched.

💬

Nothing Gets Left Behind

Every audio track keeps its language and default flag, chapters come along, and text subtitles become selectable tracks in QuickTime's Subtitles menu.

⚡

Right Beside the Original

Episode.mkv gets an Episode.mp4 next to it, ready to open any time. A file Milktoast did not create is never overwritten — and your original is only ever read.

🔒

Asks For Nothing

No network access, no analytics, no Automation permission, and no blanket file access — opening a movie from Finder is all the permission it needs.

It tells you what it did

Milktoast explains every decision it makes about your tracks, in the app and on the command line. Nothing happens to your original file.

$ milktoast --plan Episode.mkv

Episode.mkv:
Duration: 3760.9s
Mode: stream copy (fast — limited by disk speed)
Container: .mp4
  video  h264 → copy
  audio  eac3 [eng] → copy
  subs   subrip [eng] → tx3g timed text

# --plan touches nothing. Drop the flag to prepare it and play.
